The work

Records that had to sound right the first time.

Over twenty years I have mixed and mastered for more than 200 artists. Some of it was pop built for radio. Some of it was classical voice, which leaves nowhere to hide. Some of it was club music made to move a room at three in the morning.

Two names

Meraj Mirzaei is the engineer. MIRAGE is the artist.

Everything I mix and master carries my own name. Since 2025 I also release my own music as MIRAGE — deep house and electronic tracks built around Eastern instruments and textures, alongside pop and trap.
